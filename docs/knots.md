## Knots on Solana: a meme coin whose tax pays you in its own mirror image

![KNOTS on Solana: what is live today versus what is documented but not yet proven](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AlexBotlord/web3-earner-assets/main/knots/knots-card-2026-10-17.png)

$KNOTS is $STONK spelled backwards — same letters, other side of the wall. That is the entire pitch, and it is also the most interesting thing about the token: its main liquidity pair is with the asset it reverses, and its redistribution mechanism pays holders in that same mirrored asset.

## What Knots actually is

KNOTS ON STONK is a Solana token launched through the StonkFun Launchpad. The mint is `8RVBk8vxLiUHueLUW1f4izFVqN3nWippLhkohKg6EGkS`, and its primary market is a KNOTS/STONK pool on Raydium (`BUVzsLLLG7GWoyJVoU31pXiBveazA6GXTavZ9VD3CwS9`), paired against $STONK (`6GmAFSYs4gk3FDao5FzzySQpPZaWsa4rUJHacpMpUNgx`). The pool has existed since early September 2026 ([DexScreener](https://dexscreener.com/solana/buvzslllg7gwoyjvou31pxibveaza6gxtavz9vd3cws9), pair creation timestamp, observed 17 Oct 2026).

The project is not only a ticker joke. Around the token sit a small browser mini-game ("Reverse jump"), a Trading Cup with a 50,000 STONK prize pool, and a "Kickback" viewer that claims to show how much $STONK your $KNOTS has paid you. The site also carries terms, privacy and disclaimer pages, and is operated by KNOTS ON STONK LLC (Wyoming, USA) — unusual housekeeping for a coin this young ([knotsonstonk.com](https://www.knotsonstonk.com/), 17 Oct 2026).

Who is it for? Solana meme-coin traders who care less about a roadmap and more about a mechanical, checkable payout loop.

## The token mechanic

KNOTS charges a **3% transfer tax on every transfer, on any venue**. According to the project, that tax is paid out to holders **in $STONK, pro-rata**, with **~2.5% deducted to cover the network costs of distributing and supporting operations**. Distribution happens only to wallets holding at least **$20 of $KNOTS** at the moment of payout ([knotsonstonk.com](https://www.knotsonstonk.com/), 17 Oct 2026).

## One promising aspect

The payout is verifiable rather than aspirational. Every transfer is taxed on-chain, and the distributions land in $STONK — a token that exists independently of this project. You do not have to trust a roadmap slide; you can watch the flow. The mirrored-pair design also gives the coin a real, two-sided relationship with an existing market instead of a generic SOL pair.

## One risk worth asking about

Read the fee wording carefully: if the ~2.5% deduction is taken out of the 3% tax itself, roughly **0.5% of each transfer would reach holders**, with the rest covering distribution and operations. And when I checked on 17 Oct 2026, the site's own counters for "paid to holders", "waiting to distribute" and "payouts" returned **n/a** — the live figures did not load. So an outside observer currently cannot confirm how much $STONK has actually reached holders, or how often. That is the single question I would put to the team before treating the reward loop as proven.
